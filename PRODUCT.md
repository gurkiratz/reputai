# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agents and managers at multi-location dental groups. They're clinic/front-office staff or practice managers, not designers or technical users, working from a desktop during the workday in short, repeated sessions (checking the inbox between patients, clearing a backlog at the end of the day). Agents triage reviews and draft replies; managers additionally approve, edit the brand voice, and check reports. The tool touches patient-privacy-sensitive content, so trust and correctness matter as much as speed.

## Product Purpose

A review operations platform for dental service organizations: sync Google reviews across locations, triage them by real urgency (not just star rating), draft on-brand replies with AI, block anything that would violate patient privacy before a human ever sees it, route what needs a manager, and report on response time and sentiment. Success looks like an agent clearing their inbox faster with more confidence, not a flashier dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Efficient and no-nonsense. Get in, understand the review, draft or approve, get out. Steady and quietly trustworthy given the patient-privacy stakes, never playful or decorative for its own sake. Confidence comes from clarity and speed, not from visual flourish.

## Anti-references

Explicitly not a generic AI-wrapper SaaS dashboard: no stat-card grids, no boxes-within-boxes for every piece of content, no gradient accents, no "look how much AI we have" visual noise. Not cold enterprise admin tooling either (dense gray tables, zero breathing room). The reviews/[id] page in particular should not read as a stack of bordered cards; it should read as one continuous, breathable piece of information.

## Design Principles

- Structure through typography and spacing, not borders. Reach for a box only when content genuinely needs a boundary (a distinct, self-contained action panel), not as a default container for every section.
- One primary action visible at a time. The page should always make it obvious what the agent or manager does next, not present a wall of equally-weighted controls.
- Density serves speed, not decoration. Every element on screen should shorten the time to "reply sent," not just fill space.
- Confidence through restraint. No gradients, no stat-card cliches, no borrowed SaaS visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: color contrast, full keyboard navigation, and screen reader support. No additional requirements beyond that at this time.
